# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

VaMender welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Be considerate,
assume good faith, accept correction, and focus criticism on ideas and code.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct directed at others,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Respect creator rights. Do not share paid, private, or unlicensed content, and
do not pressure maintainers to bypass access controls or licensing terms.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their
GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect
the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
